How it plays

Water falls. Steam rises. Every level asks you to use both.

  1. Steam

    Tap a vent and steam climbs, drifts and spreads under the rock. It fades if nothing catches it.

  2. Cold line

    Draw a line of rime with your finger. When you let go it drops like a solid thing and rests where it lands.

  3. Rain

    Steam that touches the line beads into drops. The drops run to the low end and fall from its tip.

  4. Pitcher

    Fill every pitcher plant to its mark at the same time. Water that lands on a hot stone boils back into steam, so even a trap can feed the rain.

Questions

Are there ads?
No. Not between levels, not for rewards, not at all.
What does Dewpitch collect?
Nothing. Your progress and your purchase stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
I'm stuck on a level.
Use a Hint to see a faint reference line. You start with two and earn more by playing.
Will my progress move to a new phone?
No, progress is saved on the device. Your purchase comes back with Settings → Restore purchases.
Do I need a connection?
Not to play. Buying and restoring go through the App Store.
