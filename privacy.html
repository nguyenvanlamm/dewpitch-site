Privacy Policy

Dewpitch · last updated November 6, 2026

Dewpitch is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, it collects no data about you, and it shares nothing with anyone.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: the levels you have cleared, your best dew-drop score on each, how many Hints and Refills you hold, and which first-clear gifts you have received.
  • Whether you have bought The whole highlands, with the App Store's transaction ID so one purchase is never counted twice.

This is stored on your device only, in the app's own local storage. Deleting the app removes it. Nothing is sent to us: Dewpitch has no server and no account.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

Chapter I is free. The whole highlands is an optional in-app purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or scripts. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser requests them from Google's servers, which see your IP address as with any web request (Google's privacy policy applies). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
